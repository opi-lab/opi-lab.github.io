summary: We present a calibration approach for multimodal 3D structured-light systems that uses digital features generated by a screen, a mirror, and an auxiliary camera, eliminating the need for specialized physical targets. Experimental results with a thermal camera demonstrate 3D reconstruction accuracy comparable to conventional techniques while reducing costs and avoiding target fabrication defects.
